Acme Corp Billing and Plans

PLAN TIERS
The Basic plan is 19 USD per user per month and includes email support and 10 GB of storage per user. The Pro plan is 49 USD per user per month and adds live chat support, 100 GB per user, and audit logs. The Enterprise plan is custom priced and adds SSO, unlimited storage, a dedicated success manager, and a signed uptime commitment.

BILLING CYCLES
Monthly plans bill on the same calendar day each month, or the last day of the month for dates that do not exist. Annual plans bill once on the signup anniversary and carry a 20 percent discount against the monthly rate. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately with a prorated credit applied.

ADDING AND REMOVING SEATS
Seats added mid-cycle are prorated to the day and charged on the next invoice. Seats removed mid-cycle take effect at the end of the current billing period; no refund is issued for the remainder. Annual plans cannot reduce seat count mid-term.

PAYMENT METHODS
Credit card and ACH transfer are accepted on all plans. Invoicing with net 30 terms is available on Enterprise plans and on annual Pro plans above 10,000 USD. Failed payments are retried on days 1, 3, and 7. After the third failure the account moves to read-only until payment succeeds.

TAXES
Prices exclude tax. Applicable sales tax or VAT is calculated at checkout based on the billing address. Customers with a valid exemption certificate should submit it to billing before the first invoice; exemptions are not applied retroactively to past invoices.

OVERAGES
Storage above the plan allowance is charged at 0.10 USD per GB per month. Customers are notified by email at 80 percent and 100 percent of their allowance. There is no hard cap; service continues and the overage appears on the next invoice.

DISCOUNTS
Registered nonprofits receive 30 percent off any plan on application. Educational institutions receive 50 percent off. Discounts do not stack with annual prepayment discounts; the larger of the two applies.

INVOICES AND RECEIPTS
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are available in account settings for 7 years. Changing the billing contact does not resend historical invoices.

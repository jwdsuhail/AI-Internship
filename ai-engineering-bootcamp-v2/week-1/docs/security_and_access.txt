Acme Corp Security and Access Policy

ACCOUNT CREDENTIALS
All employee accounts require multi-factor authentication. Hardware security keys are mandatory for anyone with production access; authenticator apps are acceptable for everyone else. SMS codes are not permitted as a second factor. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are checked against a breached-password list at creation time. Password rotation is not required on a schedule; rotation is triggered only by a suspected compromise.

PRODUCTION ACCESS
Production database access requires an approved access request that names a specific reason and an expiry date. The maximum duration for a single grant is 7 days. Standing production access is limited to the on-call rotation and the platform team. All production queries are logged and retained for 400 days.

DEVICE MANAGEMENT
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and the endpoint agent installed. Personal devices may access email and chat but not source code or customer data. A lost or stolen device must be reported within 1 hour so it can be remotely wiped. Devices that miss security updates for more than 14 days are automatically blocked from internal services.

THIRD PARTY SOFTWARE
New third-party tools that process customer data require a security review before purchase. The review takes 5 to 10 business days and covers data handling, subprocessors, and breach history. Free tools are not exempt. Browser extensions with access to page content are prohibited on work devices.

INCIDENT RESPONSE
Suspected security incidents are reported to the security channel immediately, not to a manager first. The on-call security engineer acknowledges within 30 minutes at any hour. Severity 1 incidents, meaning confirmed unauthorized access to customer data, trigger the executive notification path within 2 hours. A written postmortem is required within 5 business days of resolution.

ACCESS REVIEWS
Access to all systems is reviewed quarterly. Managers confirm each report's access list; anything unconfirmed within 10 business days is revoked automatically. Departing employees have all access revoked within 1 hour of their final day ending.

VULNERABILITY DISCLOSURE
External researchers may report vulnerabilities to the published security address. Acknowledgement is sent within 3 business days. Critical findings are patched within 7 days, high within 30 days, and medium within 90 days.

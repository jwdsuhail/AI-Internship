Acme Corp Employee Handbook

REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with written manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require director sign-off and are reviewed every quarter. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. Remote work from outside the country of employment is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and must be registered with HR in advance.

VACATION AND PAID LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued monthly at 1.67 days per month. Unused leave rolls over into the following year up to a maximum of 5 days; anything above that is forfeited on 31 January. Leave requests of 5 consecutive days or more require 3 weeks notice. Public holidays follow the calendar of the employee's registered work location and do not count against the annual allowance.

SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from annual leave. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick days do not roll over.

EXPENSES
Receipts are required for any expense over 25 USD. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date; submissions after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Travel bookings must go through the corporate travel portal. The daily meal allowance is 60 USD for domestic travel and 85 USD for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

EQUIPMENT
Each employee receives a laptop refresh every 3 years. Monitors, keyboards, and other peripherals are ordered through the IT portal and do not require manager approval below 300 USD. Home office stipend is 500 USD, available once every 2 years. Damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 5 business days.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Formal performance reviews occur twice per year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment, and peer feedback from at least 2 colleagues. Promotion decisions are made only during the December cycle.

TRAINING BUDGET
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 USD for courses, conferences, and books. Unused training budget does not roll over. Requests above 800 USD require director approval.
